Yok Makes a Mistake

Inside the story

Practice the language of truth, apology, and repair in two languages.

A paint spill damages Tibo’s cardboard dinosaur. Yok wants to hide, but she chooses truth, apology, and repair instead—and learns that being brave does not mean never making mistakes.

After a paint spill damages Tibo’s cardboard dinosaur, Yok wants to hide. The story follows the more courageous path—tell the truth, apologize, and help repair—while giving bilingual families useful language for a very common childhood moment.

Made for shared readingAges 3–6 · English + French
