Yok’s Tiny Roar

Even a small voice can help someone.

Published: July 12, 2026.

Buy on Amazon ↗All books →

Inside the story

A small voice can still be a useful, brave voice.

Yok discovers that a whisper, point, gesture, or one brave word can still matter.

Yok’s experience makes room for children who do not always speak loudly or quickly. The story shows several ways to communicate—words, pointing, gestures, and quiet attempts—so self-advocacy does not depend on having a big voice.

Made for shared readingAges 3–6

A gentle read for quieter children and for conversations about asking for help, speaking up, and noticing nonverbal communication.
