Yok Tries Again

Jump rope feels too hard until Yok breaks the task into one tiny piece and tries again when ready.

Buy on AmazonSee release calendarAll books →

Inside the story

Let “try again” mean smaller steps, not more pressure.

For parents, teachers, and librarians

Yok’s jump-rope challenge makes persistence feel flexible rather than forceful. Children can see that pausing, choosing one tiny piece, and returning when ready are all part of learning something difficult.

Made for shared readingAges 3–6

A gentle growth-mindset story for children who become frustrated when a new skill does not work immediately.

Why this story can help

Let “try again” mean smaller steps, not more pressure.

Yok’s jump-rope challenge makes persistence feel flexible rather than forceful. Children can see that pausing, choosing one tiny piece, and returning when ready are all part of learning something difficult.

Good for

  • Perseverance without pressure
  • Frustration with new skills
  • Breaking tasks into smaller pieces
