About the story

Preview the sensory details of a haircut and the words a child can use during it.

Yok’s pink tuft needs a trim, but she has questions about what a haircut will feel like. The stylist shows her what will happen, listens when she asks for fewer sprays, and lets Yok try unfamiliar sensations in small pieces.

This title belongs to Yok First Experiences, a six-book collection of gentle stories for young children and the grown-ups who read with them.

Why this story can help

Preview the sensory details of a haircut and the words a child can use during it.

Yok wants to know what the haircut will feel like. The stylist responds by showing what happens, listening when Yok asks for fewer sprays, and introducing sensations in small pieces—making consent and communication part of the experience.

Good for

  • First haircuts
  • Sensory uncertainty
  • Asking for adjustments during a service

After reading

Three gentle conversation starters

  1. Which haircut sensation would you want to try slowly?
  2. What could you say if there were too many sprays?
  3. How can a stylist help a new experience feel more predictable?
