About the story

A familiar moment, made easier to understand.

Yok’s pink tuft needs a trim, but she has questions about what a haircut will feel like. The stylist shows her what will happen, listens when she asks for fewer sprays, and lets Yok try unfamiliar sensations in small pieces.

This title belongs to Yok First Experiences, a six-book collection of gentle stories for young children and the grown-ups who read with them.
