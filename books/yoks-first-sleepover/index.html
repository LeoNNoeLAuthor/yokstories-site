About the story

Make room for excitement and homesickness at the same time.

At her first sleepover, Yok is excited—and then misses home. A familiar picture, a short goodnight call, Rabbit, and supportive friends help her discover that missing home and enjoying a new experience can both be true.

This title belongs to Yok First Experiences, a six-book collection of gentle stories for young children and the grown-ups who read with them.

Why this story can help

Make room for excitement and homesickness at the same time.

Yok’s first sleepover does not require her to stop missing home before she can enjoy the evening. Familiar pictures, Rabbit, supportive friends, and a brief goodnight connection show children that two feelings can coexist.

Good for

  • First sleepovers
  • Homesickness
  • Mixed feelings about being away

After reading

Three gentle conversation starters

  1. What helped Yok feel connected to home?
  2. Can you miss home and still have fun?
  3. What comfort would you want to bring for a night away?
