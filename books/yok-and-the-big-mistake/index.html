Yok and the Big Mistake

A mistake can become a chance to make things better.

Published: July 18, 2026.

Buy on Amazon ↗All books →

Inside the story

Move from “I made a mistake” to “I can help make it better.”

A paint spill feels scary, but Yok learns to tell what happened, say sorry, and help make things new.

A mistake can trigger fear before a child is ready to think about repair. Yok’s paint spill creates a simple sequence children can remember: tell what happened, say sorry, and take part in making things better.

Made for shared readingAges 3–6

A practical story for children learning that mistakes are not the end of the story—and that repair can be active, concrete, and kind.

After reading

Three gentle conversation starters

  1. Why can telling the truth after a mistake feel hard?
  2. What is the difference between saying sorry and helping repair something?
  3. What could you do after an accident at home or school?
