About the story

Give children useful words for a day that simply feels too loud.

On a day when every sound seems to pile on top of the next, Yok becomes overwhelmed. With time to breathe and words for what she needs, she learns to say “I need quiet” and finds a gentler way back into the day.

This title belongs to Yok Everyday Stories, a six-book collection of gentle stories for young children and the grown-ups who read with them.

Why this story can help

Give children useful words for a day that simply feels too loud.

When sounds pile up, Yok becomes overwhelmed before she can explain what is wrong. The story models a pause, breathing space, and the clear self-advocacy phrase “I need quiet,” making an invisible need easier to communicate.

Good for

  • Sound sensitivity and overwhelm
  • Asking for quiet
  • Recovering after overstimulation

After reading

Three gentle conversation starters

  1. How did Yok know the noise was becoming too much?
  2. What words could you use when you need a quieter space?
  3. What helps your body recover after a very noisy moment?
