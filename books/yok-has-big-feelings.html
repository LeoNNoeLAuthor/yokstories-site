Yok Has Big Feelings
Yok a de grandes émotions

Sometimes a big feeling needs one small word to make sense.

When Yok’s tall block tower crashes, her body feels hot, tight, and wordless. With patient help from her friends, she learns that one moment can hold several feelings and that naming them can help.

For parents, teachers, and bilingual families

What this story practices

Big feelings & emotional vocabulary through one small, child-sized situation.

  • Name one body clue before naming the feeling.
  • Try a feeling word in English and French.
  • Use “I need a moment” when words are not ready yet.

Bilingual reading

Two languages, one story moment.

The book presents story moments in both English and French, supported by the same characters, setting, and visual context. Read one language first, alternate pages, or revisit the second language later.
