Yok’s First Friend

One tiny brave step can begin a friendship.

Published: June 28, 2026.

Buy on Amazon ↗All books →

Inside the story

A reassuring story about shyness and making a first friend.

Yok feels different and shy at the park until Tom offers a patient, gentle invitation to play.

This story gives young children a concrete way to see friendship begin without pressure: someone notices, waits, and offers a gentle invitation. It is especially useful before a new playground, preschool group, or other social situation where joining in can feel difficult.

Made for shared readingAges 3–6

A warm choice for children who watch first, need time to join in, or are learning how to invite someone else to play.
