Yok Helps a Friend / Yok aide un ami

Helping starts with listening—and sometimes the kindest answer is simply “No, thank you.”

Inside the story

Show that good helping begins with asking and listening.

Tibo is building a little bridge when Yok rushes in to help before asking what he needs. Yok learns that being a good friend means listening, respecting another person’s answer, and understanding that “No, thank you” is perfectly okay.

Yok is eager to help Tibo, but the important social skill is not rushing in—it is finding out what he actually wants. The story makes consent, listening, and respectful helping understandable through a child-sized friendship moment.
