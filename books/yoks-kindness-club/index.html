Yok’s Kindness Club

Small kind actions can fill a big heart.

Published: August 1, 2026.

Buy on Amazon ↗All books →

Inside the story

Make kindness concrete enough for a preschooler to notice and try.

Yok learns that kindness does not have to be huge; sharing space, returning something, and sitting beside a friend count.

Instead of treating kindness as a big abstract idea, Yok notices ordinary actions children can actually repeat. The story is well suited to talking about the quiet forms of kindness that may not earn attention but still change someone’s day.

Made for shared readingAges 3–6

Ideal for kindness themes at home, preschool, kindergarten, storytime, or any moment when children are learning what caring can look like.
