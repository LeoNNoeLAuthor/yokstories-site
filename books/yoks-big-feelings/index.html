Yok’s Big Feelings

Big feelings are like weather inside the body.

Buy on Amazon ↗All books →

Inside the story

Help children connect big feelings with body clues and useful words.

Yok learns to notice body feelings, name feelings one at a time, breathe, and ask for what she needs.

Yok treats feelings like weather inside the body: something to notice rather than something to fear. The story offers a simple path from sensation to language—notice, name, breathe, and ask for what is needed.

Made for shared readingAges 3–6

A natural companion to calm-down routines, feelings vocabulary, and conversations about what emotions feel like physically.

After reading

Three gentle conversation starters

  1. Where do you notice a big feeling in your body?
  2. What words can describe more than one feeling at once?
  3. What might your body need when feelings get very big?
