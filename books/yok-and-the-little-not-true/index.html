A Yok Story · Ages 3–6

Yok and the Little Not-True

Truth can make a heavy heart feel lighter.

Published: August 8, 2026.

Buy on Amazon ↗All books →

Inside the story

Talk about honesty without turning truth-telling into a lecture.

Yok learns that telling the truth can feel hard, but true words open the way to repair.

Young children sometimes avoid the truth because they are worried about what happens next. Yok’s experience keeps the focus on the relief and repair that become possible once the true words are finally said.

Made for shared readingAges 3–6

A supportive choice for conversations about honesty, worry, trust, and what grown-ups can do to make truth-telling feel safer.
