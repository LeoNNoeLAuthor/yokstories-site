Inside this volume

Five gentle forest stories

Created for bedtime reading, peaceful moments, and small conversations between children and grown-ups.

Friends you will meet

  • Milo and Mina Mouse
  • Wisp Wolf
  • Deer
  • Picnic friends
  • Festival friends

Forest lessons to carry home

  • Inclusion often begins with a new idea.
  • Welcome can happen in more than one way.
  • Being scared does not mean a friend cannot find the way home.
  • Kindness can mean making space instead of pushing forward.
  • Shared meals become warmer when everyone belongs.

For shared reading

A soft place to pause together.

Some pages may bring a smile. Some may invite a question. Some may simply offer a quiet place to rest at the end of the day.

  1. Read: Share one story without rushing.
  2. Notice: Ask what Luma or her friend was feeling.
  3. Name: Choose one gentle lesson from the story.
  4. Carry: Try one small kind action afterward.
