Yok’s Brave First Day

Three small steps can make a new place feel smaller.

Published: July 5, 2026.

Buy on Amazon ↗All books →

Inside the story

Turn a big first day into a few manageable steps.

Yok learns that a big new place can feel safer with one small step at a time: backpack, hello, one activity.

New places can feel enormous when everything arrives at once. Yok’s simple sequence—backpack, hello, one activity—offers children and grown-ups an easy language for breaking a first day into smaller, more predictable pieces.

Made for shared readingAges 3–6

Useful before preschool, kindergarten, a new class, or any unfamiliar group where a child may benefit from one-step-at-a-time reassurance.

After reading

Three gentle conversation starters

  1. Which small step would you want to do first in a new place?
  2. What could help a new room feel more familiar?
  3. Who could you say hello to if you felt nervous?
