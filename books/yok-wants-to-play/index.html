Yok Wants to Play

Sometimes joining in starts with just one small invitation.

Inside the story

Explore the small social steps that help someone join a game.

Yok and friends are building a dinosaur train when they notice a little dinosaur watching nearby. They learn that making room, offering a gesture, and waiting kindly can help someone join in.

A child watching from nearby may want to participate but still need time. This bilingual story focuses on making room, offering a clear invitation, and then allowing the other child to decide when to join.
