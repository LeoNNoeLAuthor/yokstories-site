About the story

A quiet story for the shaky minutes after a bad dream.

After a frightening dream, Yok needs time to feel safe again. Familiar comforts, gentle reassurance, and room to talk about what happened help the scary feeling become smaller.

This title belongs to Yok Everyday Stories, a six-book collection of gentle stories for young children and the grown-ups who read with them.

Why this story can help

A quiet story for the shaky minutes after a bad dream.

A scary dream can end while the frightened feeling stays. Yok’s experience validates that lingering fear and focuses on familiar comfort, gentle reassurance, and talking through what happened until the body begins to feel safe again.

Good for

  • Nightmares and bedtime fears
  • Reassurance after waking
  • Returning to a sense of safety
