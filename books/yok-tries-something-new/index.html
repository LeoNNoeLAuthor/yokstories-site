About the story

Make “try it” feel like an invitation rather than a demand.

At the climbing wall, Yok faces the uncomfortable moment before trying something unfamiliar. With encouragement and permission to go at her own pace, she discovers that trying can begin with one small step.

This title belongs to Yok Everyday Stories, a six-book collection of gentle stories for young children and the grown-ups who read with them.

Why this story can help

Make “try it” feel like an invitation rather than a demand.

At the climbing wall, Yok is allowed to approach the unfamiliar experience in pieces. The story supports a flexible kind of courage: look, pause, choose a small step, and decide what comes next instead of being rushed into the whole challenge.

Good for

  • Trying unfamiliar activities
  • Courage at a child’s own pace
  • Reducing pressure around first attempts
