About the story

Help a new caregiver feel less unfamiliar before the evening begins.

A new grown-up named Mina is staying with Yok for the evening. Clear plans, time to warm up, familiar routines, and chances for Yok to be the expert help a new person become less unfamiliar.

This title belongs to Yok First Experiences, a six-book collection of gentle stories for young children and the grown-ups who read with them.

Why this story can help

Help a new caregiver feel less unfamiliar before the evening begins.

Mina is new to Yok, so the evening works best when the plan is clear and familiar routines remain visible. Giving Yok time to warm up—and chances to be the expert on her own home—helps the relationship start without forcing instant comfort.

Good for

  • Meeting a new babysitter
  • Separation at home
  • Using routines to create predictability

After reading

Three gentle conversation starters

  1. What would you want a new babysitter to know about your routine?
  2. Why can showing someone around help you feel more comfortable?
  3. What part of the evening would you want planned ahead?
