Sometimes the bravest words are the ones that are not perfect yet.

At a Friendship Picnic, everyone will say a sentence in English and French. Yok and Tibo each know one language more easily than the other, so they practice together and discover that words do not have to be perfect to connect.

For parents, teachers, and bilingual families

What this story practices

Language learning, confidence & friendship through one small, child-sized situation.

  • Practice one short sentence in both languages.
  • Celebrate trying before pronunciation feels perfect.
  • Let friends help each other find the words.

Bilingual reading

Two languages, one story moment.

The book presents story moments in both English and French, supported by the same characters, setting, and visual context. Read one language first, alternate pages, or revisit the second language later.
