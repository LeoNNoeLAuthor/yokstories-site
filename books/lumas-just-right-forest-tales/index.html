Why this story can help

Five stories about choices that are thoughtful rather than simply “right.”

Across five forest adventures, Luma meets questions of sharing, planning, honesty, strength, and balance. The stories invite children to notice how different choices feel and why “just right” can depend on the moment.

Good for

  • Sharing and honest choices
  • Planning and balance
  • Talking through decisions together

Three gentle conversation starters
