How to read them

Use one language, both languages, or switch as your child is ready.

The illustrations carry the story while repeated moments in English and French help children connect meaning without turning reading time into a lesson.

  1. Read: Enjoy the story first in the language that feels most comfortable.
  2. Repeat: Revisit the paired moment in the second language.
  3. Notice: Point out one useful word or phrase.
  4. Try: Let children speak, point, gesture, or simply listen.
