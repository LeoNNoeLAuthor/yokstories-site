Inside the kit

Read, talk, practice, and keep it child-led.

Each eight-page kit includes a read-aloud guide, a parent conversation card, practice cards, a printable certificate, a coloring page, four bookmarks, and Yok’s Little Try. Use one language first if that keeps the story flowing, then revisit the paired phrases on another reading.

Themes: language confidence, asking for repetition, slowing down, imperfect pronunciation, friendship, and brave communication.

Works well for

  • English–French shared reading and language practice
  • Home, classroom, or library storytime
  • Gentle bilingual conversation prompts after the book
