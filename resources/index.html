These free printables connect directly to common early-childhood moments: making a friend, handling a mistake, waiting, naming big feelings, trying again, and practicing kindness. Choose one resource, print it, and use only what feels helpful.

Using the library

Keep it simple.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Each resource page links directly to its free PDF download.

Who are the printables for?

Most are designed around the same ages as the original Yok stories—roughly ages 3–6—with grown-up support as needed.

Do I need the book first?

No. The stand-alone activities work independently. Storytime kits and coloring books are most meaningful when paired with their related Yok story.
