Inside the kit

Read, talk, practice, and keep it child-led.

Each eight-page kit includes a read-aloud guide, a parent conversation card, practice cards, a printable certificate, a coloring page, four bookmarks, and Yok’s Little Try. Pick the part that fits your child or group and leave the rest for another day.

Themes: making choices, waiting, unfamiliar food, asking for small changes, movement breaks, and restaurant confidence.

Works well for

  • Home or classroom read-aloud follow-up
  • Library or small-group storytime
  • Gentle conversation prompts after the book
