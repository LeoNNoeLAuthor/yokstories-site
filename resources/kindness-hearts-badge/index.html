Kindness activity · Ages 3–6

Kindness Hearts

Kindness can be easier for young children to understand when it is attached to something concrete. These printable hearts can help families or groups notice small caring actions without turning kindness into a competition.

Free download · No sign-up required

Ideas for using it

Print it, use it, and let the conversation stay gentle.

This printable is designed as a simple extension of shared reading—not a worksheet children need to complete perfectly. Pick the part that fits your child or group and leave the rest for another day.

Works well for

  • Kindness themes at home or preschool
  • A follow-up after a friendship story
  • Noticing quiet caring actions during the week
