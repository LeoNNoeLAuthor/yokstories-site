Social-emotional printable · Ages 3–6

Feelings Weather Chart

Use weather as a simple visual language for emotions: sunny, cloudy, stormy, or somewhere in between. The chart gives young children a low-pressure way to point, name, and talk about how they feel.

Ideas for using it

Print it, use it, and let the conversation stay gentle.

This printable is designed as a simple extension of shared reading—not a worksheet children need to complete perfectly. Pick the part that fits your child or group and leave the rest for another day.

Works well for

  • Morning or bedtime emotional check-ins
  • Classroom feelings conversations
  • A visual prompt when words are hard to find
